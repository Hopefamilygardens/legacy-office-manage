# Governance

This folder documents the roles, responsibilities, and decision-making processes for Legacy Office Manager.

## Purpose

- Define who owns what (roles and accountabilities)
- Document how decisions are made and escalated
- Record team structure and communication norms
- Establish policies for contributions, reviews, and access

## Governing Authority

The **Constitution** (`../constitution/LEGACY_OS_CONSTITUTION.md`) is the highest authority. All governance policies must align with it.
