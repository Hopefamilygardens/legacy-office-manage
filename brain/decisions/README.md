# Decisions

This folder contains Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) and other significant decision logs for Legacy Office Manager.

## Purpose

- Record architectural and strategic decisions with their context and rationale
- Track the status of decisions (proposed, accepted, superseded, deprecated)
- Provide a permanent record to avoid relitigating past decisions
- Help new contributors understand why things are the way they are

## Format

Each decision should be a separate Markdown file, named with a sequential number and a short title, e.g. `001-use-sveltekit.md`.

## Governing Authority

The **Constitution** (`../constitution/LEGACY_OS_CONSTITUTION.md`) is the highest authority. All decisions must be consistent with the Constitution or must explicitly amend it through the proper process.
