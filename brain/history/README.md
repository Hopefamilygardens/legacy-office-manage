# History

This folder preserves the historical context, past decisions, and lessons learned for Legacy Office Manager.

## Purpose

- Record the origin story and evolution of the product
- Capture lessons learned from past projects and incidents
- Preserve context behind major pivots or changes in direction
- Serve as an institutional memory for the team

## Governing Authority

The **Constitution** (`../constitution/LEGACY_OS_CONSTITUTION.md`) is the highest authority. Historical records are preserved for context; where they conflict with current standards, the Constitution and current standards prevail.
