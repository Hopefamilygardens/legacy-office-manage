# Strategy

This folder captures the vision, goals, and strategic roadmaps for Legacy Office Manager.

## Purpose

- Document long-term product vision and mission
- Track strategic goals and OKRs
- Maintain product roadmaps and priority frameworks
- Record strategic decisions and the reasoning behind them

## Governing Authority

The **Constitution** (`../constitution/LEGACY_OS_CONSTITUTION.md`) is the highest authority. All strategic plans must align with its principles.
